AI   INTRODUCTION :

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines, enabling computers to learn, reason, problem-solve, and make decisions. By analyzing vast datasets to identify patterns, AI powers technologies like chatbots, self-driving cars, and recommendation engines. Key subfields include machine learning (learning from data) and deep learning. AI relies on algorithm, which are step by step procedure to process the data, identify patterns,and make predictions or decisions without begin explictly programmed for every scenario.  ML   (MACHINE LERANING) is a subset of AI where computers use data to improve their own performance . DEEP   LEARNING is a more advanced subset of ML (machine learning ) inspired by human brain's structure(neural network ) to process complex patterns.

AI  MERITS:

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has many advantages that make tasks easier and more efficient. It can process large amounts of data quickly, helping people make better decisions. AI also reduces human errors and can work continuously without getting tired. In many fields such as healthcare, education, and business, AI helps automate repetitive tasks, saving time and increasing productivity. Overall, AI improves efficiency, accuracy, and innovation in modern technology.

AI   DEMERITS :

Artificial Intelligence also has some disadvantages. It can lead to job loss because machines may replace human workers in many tasks. AI systems are expensive to develop and maintain. They also lack human emotions and creativity, which can affect decision-making in sensitive situations. In addition, over-dependence on AI may reduce human skills and critical thinking. Therefore, while AI is powerful, it also has some important limitations.
